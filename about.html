Who we are

Our company

Inyass Technology Solutions Ltd, formerly Inyass PC Solutions Ventures, is an Information Technology solutions company registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission as a limited liability company. Operating from Niger State and serving Nigeria at large, we provide ICT services to individuals, businesses, commercial organizations, and institutions.

Our work spans computer networking, website design and development, computer training, supply and installation of computers and modern accessories, electronic gadgets, stationery supplies, IT collaboration, printing, and publishing services. As technology needs grow, we continue to support clients of different sizes with dependable solutions and quality service delivery.
